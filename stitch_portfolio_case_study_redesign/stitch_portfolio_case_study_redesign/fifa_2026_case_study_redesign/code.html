FIFA World Cup 2026: Our Neighborhood, Your Nation

Uniting international fans with Toronto's authentic cultural neighborhoods—Little Portugal, Little Italy, Koreatown, and Little Brazil—through immersive hubs and digital storytelling.

Timeline Mar 2025
Tools Figma, PowerPoint, Social Media Analytics, Brand Strategy

Selected Campaign Outputs

Hero visual and key campaign deliverables showcasing the vibrant, multicultural identity.

The Brief & Strategy

The Organization

Toronto Metropolitan University · FIFA World Cup Challenge sought a refined communications and digital engagement strategy designed to make complex data, mission priorities, and creative storytelling immediately impactful and accessible.

The Objective & Approach

"Transforming high-level research and institutional initiatives into audience-focused narratives that drive measurable engagement."

We established structured multi-channel campaign architectures, produced bespoke visual and motion assets, and built cross-institutional feedback loops to guarantee lasting organizational resonance.

Strategic Evolution

The project progressed across four structured phases from initial audit to final performance measurement.

Stage 1

Analyzing Fan Demographics

Explored fan behavior patterns from previous World Cups and identified opportunities to leverage Canada's vibrant multicultural mosaic.

Stage 2

Cultural Identity

Developing the narrative that football is the international language, utilizing Toronto's diverse neighborhoods as physical hubs for authentic cultural engagement.

Stage 3

Digital Engagement

Structuring multi-channel campaign architectures to bring global travel content creators or streamers to share the World Cup experience across national media channels.
